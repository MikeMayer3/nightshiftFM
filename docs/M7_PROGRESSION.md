# M7 — persistent options and player progression

M7 adds the campaign-selection and equipment flow, first-clear unlocks, twelve
station modules with two slots, three saved loadouts, a recipe codex, station
color mastery and mission-medal records. Android version: **0.7.0 / code 11**.

The twelve mission entries are explicitly labeled prototype battles. They reuse
the existing ten-wave encounter. Mission-specific waves, enemies, bosses,
difficulties and Contracts belong to M8 and are not implemented here.

## Progression and reset contract

New profiles have Pulse, Capacitor, Arc Aerial, Bass Driver and Static Net.
Clearing a mission opens the next mission. Equipment unlocks are derived from
the highest contiguous first clear, without a currency or cumulative stat boost.

| First clear | Newly available options |
|---|---|
| 2 | Echo Deck and the two-slot patchboard |
| 3 | Reverb Well and Relay shield |
| 4 | Needle Swarm, Sweep, Hot Tubes and Heavy Battery |
| 5 | Long Mast and Fast Fuse |
| 6 | Burst main weapon, Signal Booster and Quiet Room |
| 7 | Wideband Module |
| 8 | Feedback shield and Narrowband Module |
| 9 | Counterweight |
| 10 | Thin Wire |
| 11 | Night Ledger |
| 12 | Glass Tower |

A new mission or Restart resets ranks to one, acquired supports, damage taken,
Signal choices, rerolls, banishes, effects and patch connections. It preserves the
chosen main/shield/starting support and zero to two selected modules. Continue
restores the same run with the equipment availability frozen at its start.
Profile-only saves do not display Continue. Legacy prototype wins remain legacy
records; they do not silently grant campaign clears.

## Modules

The immutable module resources and their localization are generated by
`tools/generate_campaign_content.py`. `ModuleStats` is shared by equipment
previews, live track derivation and restored sessions. Coefficients add before
multiplying the relevant baseline; bonuses are not repeatedly applied to a
previously derived stat.

| Module | Benefit | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Hot Tubes | +15% automatic attack rate | −20% shield recharge |
| Long Mast | +20% targeting range | −10% maximum hull |
| Heavy Battery | +25% capacity | −20% shield recharge |
| Fast Fuse | −15% shield ability cooldown | −15% capacity |
| Signal Booster | +15% additive attack damage | +1s recharge delay |
| Quiet Room | +25% shield recharge | −10% support activation rate |
| Wideband | +20% area radius | −15% direct damage |
| Narrowband | +20% direct damage | −15% area radius |
| Counterweight | +25% displacement | −10% projectile travel speed |
| Thin Wire | +30% Charged duration | −10% capacity |
| Night Ledger | One additional run reroll | −10% main damage |
| Glass Tower | +10 percentage points main crit chance | −15% maximum hull |

Direct attacks are precision shots/volleys, Arc and Needles. Sweep, Bass, Net and
Well are area attacks. The aimed Burst uses area damage/radius rules and retains
its existing five-second cooldown. Shield retaliation and independently generated
patchboard explosions receive their applicable area/damage modifiers once.
Echo inherits its recorded main attack; Quiet Room slows its event-based replay
meter. Module descriptions do not promise a second multiplier on copied damage.

## Records and migration

The atomic profile/run envelope is unchanged. Profile schema 3 adds campaign
records, presets, mastery and selected station color; schemas 1 and 2 migrate with
an empty campaign profile. Active M7 checkpoints carry content `m7.1` and a strict
mission/availability/module snapshot. Existing M3–M6 continuations retain their
original rules.

First-clear rewards are committed in the same envelope as victory. Repeated
completion of the same run cannot duplicate clears, medals or mastery. Replays
can improve best hull ratio/time and collect missing medals. The three current
medals are Clear, No Breach and Intact Hull; bespoke M8 mission medals are later.
A completed rank-8 support unlocks its station color. Three distinct completed
capstones earn a Full Range title in the mastery record. Neither changes stats.

Presets copy their selections independently. They reject locked, duplicated or
excessive modules. Migration maps known legacy equipment aliases and removes
retired/unavailable preset options with a visible migration notice. Unknown IDs
inside an active run fail validation and use the existing recoverable-save flow;
they are not silently replaced during combat. Provided-save migration fixtures
verify persistence, not automatic recovery following an actual uninstall.
