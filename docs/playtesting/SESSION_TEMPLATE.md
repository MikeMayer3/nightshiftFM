## Participant feedback sheet

There are no right answers. You can skip any question.

1. What do you think the goal of the game is?
2. Which choice changed how you played the most, if any?
3. Was anything confusing, hard to see, or awkward to control?
4. What do you think caused your result? (Skip if the run did not end.)
5. What was the best moment? Was there a point where you wanted to stop?
6. What would you want to try or unlock next, if anything?
7. In your own words, what did the shield and mixer do? “Not sure” is useful.
8. How interested would you be in playing again on your own? Why?
9. Asked last: If the complete game cost about $2 with no ads or extra purchases,
   would you consider buying it? Why or why not? What would you need to see first?

Interest and purchase answers are stated preferences, not measured retention or
sales. Do not substitute this answer for observed spontaneous retry behavior.
